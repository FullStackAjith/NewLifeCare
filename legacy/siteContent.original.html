Please contact the centre before visiting to confirm its current list of required documents and permitted personal items. Do not assume a standard list applies.

Ask the team to explain its privacy policy, consent process, who can receive updates, and any legal or safety-related limits before sharing sensitive information.

Aftercare is listed as a support topic, but availability and format should be confirmed with the centre team.
